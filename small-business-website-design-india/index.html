Frequently Asked Questions

Which website is best for a small business?

Most small businesses benefit from a focused 5–10 page website with service pages, trust information, location details and clear enquiry actions. The right platform depends on how often content changes and whether online selling is required.

Can customers contact my business on WhatsApp?

Yes. A configured WhatsApp button can open a pre-filled message, helping customers start an enquiry quickly from mobile or desktop.

Is SEO included in a small business website?

SiteArvo includes SEO-ready technical foundations such as headings, metadata, responsive structure and crawlable pages. Ongoing rankings require useful content, authority and regular optimization.

Can I add more pages later?

Yes. A maintainable website structure makes it possible to add services, industries, guides and other useful pages as the business grows.
