Website Development Cost in India: A Practical 2026 Guide

Website cost depends on scope—not only page count. This guide explains realistic planning ranges, cost drivers and questions to ask before choosing a package or quotation.

Typical website development cost ranges in India

Indicative planning ranges can help create an initial budget, but the final quote should always reflect confirmed requirements. A focused landing page may fall around ₹8,000–₹20,000. A professional small-business website commonly ranges from ₹15,000–₹40,000. E-commerce projects may begin around ₹35,000 and exceed ₹1,25,000 as catalogue, payment, shipping and admin requirements grow. Custom web applications often start around ₹75,000 and increase with workflows, integrations and security needs.

What changes the final price?

  • Number and uniqueness of pages
  • Custom design versus adapted design system
  • Content writing and image preparation
  • Product catalogue and e-commerce workflows
  • Forms, payments, bookings and third-party APIs
  • Admin panel or content management requirements
  • Authentication, roles and stored customer data
  • SEO depth, analytics and ongoing maintenance

Fixed package or custom quotation?

A fixed package works best when the scope is predictable: a defined number of pages, standard forms and clearly listed inclusions. A custom quotation is more appropriate for e-commerce rules, dashboards, customer accounts, integrations or workflows that need discovery before development. Starting-from prices are useful for planning but should not be treated as a final commitment without scope confirmation.

Questions to ask before approving a proposal

  • Which pages and features are included?
  • Who provides final text, images and product data?
  • How many revision rounds are included?
  • Are domain, hosting, paid plugins and subscriptions separate?
  • Will mobile, speed and browser testing be completed?
  • Who owns the final assets and source code?
  • What support is available after launch?

Choosing value instead of only the lowest number

A cheaper website can become expensive if it is slow, difficult to update or missing essential enquiry paths. Compare the scope, communication, implementation quality and support—not just the headline price. Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a 5-page website cost in India?

A professional 5-page website often falls around ₹15,000–₹30,000 depending on design, content, forms, SEO setup and revisions. Fixed packages may be lower or higher depending on exact inclusions.

Is domain and hosting included in website development cost?

Not always. Domain registration, hosting, business email, premium plugins and third-party subscriptions should be listed separately unless the proposal explicitly includes them.

Why do e-commerce websites cost more?

E-commerce work may include product structure, payments, shipping, taxes, customer accounts, order workflows, integrations and additional testing, which increases scope.

Should I choose the cheapest website package?

Choose the smallest package that fully supports your business goal. Confirm responsive design, ownership, essential integrations, support and exclusions before comparing prices.
