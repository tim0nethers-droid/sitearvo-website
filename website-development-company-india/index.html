How much does website development cost in India?

The cost depends on page count, design depth, content, integrations and functionality. SiteArvo publishes fixed-price and starting-from options on the Pricing page, with custom work quoted after requirements are reviewed.

How long does a business website take?

A focused website can often be completed in one to three weeks when content and feedback are ready. Larger stores and custom applications require a project-specific timeline.

Can SiteArvo redesign an existing website?

Yes. Existing websites can be reviewed for structure, design, responsiveness, speed, conversion flow and search foundations before a redesign plan is prepared.

Will the website work on Hostinger?
